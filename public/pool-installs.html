Frequently asked questions

Everything Brisbane homeowners ask before getting their pool quotes.

Pool costs in Brisbane vary by type and size. Fibreglass pools typically range from $30,000–$60,000 fully installed. Concrete pools start around $50,000 and can exceed $100,000 for large custom builds. Plunge pools generally start from $20,000. Getting 3 quotes is the best way to understand what your specific project will cost.
Fibreglass pools are the fastest — most are installed within 4–8 weeks from council approval. Concrete pools take longer due to custom construction, typically 8–16 weeks. Your builder will give you a specific timeline based on your site conditions and current workload.
