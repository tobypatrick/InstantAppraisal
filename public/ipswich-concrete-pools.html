Concrete pool builders in Ipswich

A custom concrete pool is the premium choice for Ipswich homeowners who want full control over the shape, size, depth, and finish of their pool. Unlike fibreglass pools that come in pre-moulded shapes, a concrete pool is built in your backyard from scratch — which means it can be any shape, any size, and any design you want.

Concrete pools are particularly popular in established Ipswich suburbs like Leichhardt, Brassall, and Booval, where larger block sizes and mature landscaping lend themselves to a bespoke pool design. They're also a strong choice for homeowners with awkward block shapes, slopes, or specific design requirements that a standard fibreglass shell can't accommodate.

Why choose a concrete pool in Ipswich?

  • Completely custom: Any shape, any depth, any finish — freeform, lap, geometric, or fully custom
  • Built to fit your block: Ideal for awkward blocks, slopes, or irregular dimensions
  • Fully bespoke finishes: Pebblecrete, exposed aggregate, ceramic tiles, or glass mosaic
  • Long lifespan: A well-built concrete pool in Ipswich will last 30–50+ years with proper maintenance
  • Adds significant property value: A premium concrete pool increases home value more than a comparable fibreglass install

How long does concrete pool construction take in Ipswich?

Concrete pool construction in Ipswich takes longer than fibreglass because the pool is built on-site rather than delivered as a shell. Most Ipswich concrete pool builds take:

  • Ipswich City Council approval: 3–6 weeks
  • Excavation: 1–2 weeks
  • Steel frame & shotcrete: 2–4 weeks
  • Plumbing, electrical, and fittings: 2–3 weeks
  • Rendering/tiling & coping: 2–4 weeks
  • Final fill, chemical balance, handover: 1 week

Total construction time from council approval: typically 8–16 weeks. Most Ipswich homeowners are swimming 11–22 weeks after signing a contract.

Concrete pool finishes available in Ipswich

Your Ipswich pool builder will offer a range of interior finishes for your concrete pool:

  • Pebblecrete: The most popular and durable finish across Ipswich. Textured, slip-resistant, and available in multiple colours
  • Exposed aggregate: Premium pebble finish with a smooth feel. More expensive but highly durable
  • Ceramic tiles: Premium option with endless colour choices. Requires more maintenance but offers a luxury finish
  • Glass mosaic tiles: The top-end choice. Stunning finish, highest cost
